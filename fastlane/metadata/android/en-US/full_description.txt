BWeather gives you a clear, fast forecast for where you are and the places you care about. It's free, with no ads and no account.

• Current conditions, hourly and 7-day forecasts, with a temperature and rain chart
• "Warmer than usual" comparisons with the last 20 years
• Minute-by-minute rain for the next 2 hours (optional)
• Rain radar for the past 2 hours
• Air quality, UV index and pollen
• Sunrise, sunset, day length and moon phase
• Daily tips: umbrella, what to wear, sunscreen, laundry, driving
• Morning briefing, rain alerts and severe-weather alerts
• Home-screen widget
• Animated sky backgrounds, light and dark themes
• Save cities, reorder them, and swipe to remove
• Works on phones and tablets
• In English, French, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Hausa, Yorùbá and Igbo

Privacy first: your location is only used to fetch the forecast. Saved places stay on your device.
